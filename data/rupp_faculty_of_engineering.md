### Vision
To pursue an excellence in teaching, research and community engagement in Engineering Education to address the challenges of ever-changing society and globalization.

### Mission
- **Deliver Comprehensive and Dynamic Education:** covers core engineering principles, advanced topics, and interdisciplinary studies to build a solid foundation of knowledge and skills.
- **Foster Advancement in Research:** promotes innovation, collaboration and ethical practices; encourages faculty and students to think outside the box through project-based learning, creative-thinking methods and real-world challenges; gives access to research projects, internships and industry collaboration.
- **Cultivate a Global Perspective:** prepares students for the global engineering landscape through international exchange programs and collaborations with global institutions.
- **Enhance Soft Skills:** develops communication, teamwork, leadership and problem-solving through group projects, presentations and student-organization roles.
- **Encourage Lifelong Learning:** promotes continuous education and professional development in emerging technologies, trends and best practices.
- **Engage with the Community:** involves students in community service and outreach so they understand the societal impact of their work.

### Skills the faculty offers for market demand
- **Hard skills:** Telecommunication & Electronic Engineering, Information Technology Engineering, Bio-Engineering
- **Soft skills:** Communication, Team Work, Problem Solving, Innovation and Creativity
- **English:** 4 hours per week for 3 years, with a certificate of completion

### Dean's Message (Dr. Nguonphan Pheakdey)
> On behalf of all our faculty, staff and students, welcome to the Faculty of Engineering! The Faculty of Engineering, Royal University of Phnom Penh was set up on May, 07 2013 to respond to the increase demand of the industries and private sectors in the country for qualified engineers. The faculty, with four departments, offers now seven degrees programs. We thrive to bring international experiences and new innovative engineering education such as Conceive, Design, Implement, Operate (CDIO) framework to enhance existing programs and building new programs toward international level. We ventured into offering international programs in Food technology & Engineering, Data science & Engineering, 2+2 international program in Automation and Supply Chain System Engineering, and Environmental Engineering. Our engineering programs prepare our students equipped with positive mental attitude, intellectual, entrepreneurship and leadership capacity necessary to meet the challenges of the ever-changing society. With dedication, enthusiasm and hard work, the faculty has been able to produce graduates who are capable of addressing the requirement of the industries and private sectors. The faculty is dedicated to pursue and excellent teaching and research culture to deliver high quality education.
