# Data science student profile: facts

Restated from Data_Science_Student_Profile.docx as one fact per sentence.

## What a data science major is

A data science major works at the intersection of statistical mathematics, advanced computing, and domain-specific decision making.
Data science students are trained to turn massive, unorganized datasets into clear, actionable narratives that drive growth and innovation.

## Data science academic pillars

The mathematics data science needs is linear algebra, multivariable calculus, probability models, and inferential statistics.
The computer science skills data science students need are software design patterns, algorithmic optimization, data structures, and database management systems (SQL, NoSQL, and vector stores like Qdrant).
In machine learning, data science students build baseline prototypes, refine data preprocessing pipelines, run feature engineering experiments, and serve production models with MLflow.
The tool data science students use to manage the machine learning model lifecycle is MLflow.

## Data science technical toolkit

The programming languages used in data science are Python, R, SQL, C++, and Bash/Shell scripting.
The data frameworks data science students use are Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch, and TensorFlow.
The infrastructure and storage tools data science students use are Docker, Git/GitHub, PostgreSQL, and the Qdrant vector database.
The vector database data science students use is Qdrant.
The analysis environments data science students use are Jupyter Notebooks, Google Colab, VS Code, and Linux/CLI.

## Data science mindset and vision

Interdisciplinary synthesis means bridging software development pipelines with strategic financial metrics or operational goals.
Sustainable systems design is a passion for directing artificial intelligence and analytics toward sustainable community development, resource management, and efficient local infrastructure.
Iterative innovation means approaching engineering challenges through progressive experimentation, from a small local prototype or mock-up up to a scalable deployment.
The professional vision of a data science student is to use AI and analytics for sustainable community development and efficient local infrastructure.
