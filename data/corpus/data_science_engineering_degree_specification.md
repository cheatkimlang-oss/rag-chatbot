# Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE)
**Department of Information Technology Engineering**  
**Faculty of Engineering (FE) | Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)**

---

## 1. Program Overview & Introduction

The **Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE)** program prepares engineers to leverage modern computing technologies, mathematical modeling, and distributed systems to design and develop cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Big Data solutions.

* **Core Objectives:** Deliver rigorous training in programming, statistical analysis, scalable cloud infrastructure, and intelligent systems design.
* **Hands-on Practicum:** Students complete multi-semester **Project Practicum** sessions starting from Year 2 through Year 4, developing working prototypes, data pipelines, and intelligent software to solve industry challenges.
* **Institutional Support:** Curriculum frameworks and advanced research collaborations are supported via international partnerships including KOICA and GIST (Republic of Korea).

---

## 2. Research Clusters & Career Prospects

### Research Domains
* **Web and Cloud Technology:** Scalable cloud-native architectures and distributed systems.
* **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Applied ML algorithms, predictive models, and intelligent agents.
* **Big Data:** Distributed computing frameworks, ETL pipelines, and web mining.
* **Business Intelligence:** Process optimization, marketing metrics, and data-informed decision automation.

### Career & Job Opportunities
Graduates are qualified to step into roles including:
* Data Analyst
* Business Intelligence Analyst (BI Analyst)
* Associate Data Engineer
* Data Engineer
* AI / Machine Learning Engineer (AI/ML Engineer)

## 3. Evaluation and Grading System

Academic performance is evaluated at the end of each semester on a 4.00 Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) scale.

| Score Range | GPA | Letter Grade | Academic Standing |

| **80 – 84** | 3.5 | B+ | Very Good |
| **70 – 79** | 3.0 | B | Good |
| **65 – 69** | 2.5 | C+ | Fairly Good |
| **50 – 64** | 2.0 | C | Fair |
| **45 – 49** | 1.5 | D | Poor |
| **< 40** | 0.0 | E | Fail |

---

## 4. Last Semester Optional Tracks (Year 4, Semester II)

Students choose one of three specialized pathways in their final semester:
1. **Course Work Track:** Designed for students completing structured coursework alongside their final capstone project.
2. **Research Thesis Track:** Tailored for high-achieving students conducting an academic research project under direct faculty supervision.
3. **Extended Training Track:** Designed for students participating in a full-semester cooperative training program embedded within external companies or industry partner organizations.
