## Department of Information Technology Engineering

The Department of Information Technology Engineering is part of the Faculty of Engineering at the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP).
The vision of the Department of Information Technology Engineering is: Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering program at FE is designed to provide high-quality Engineers in Information Technology in Cambodia and in regional.
The Department of Information Technology Engineering offers these bachelor degrees: Information Technology Engineering Bachelor Degree, Data Science And Engineering Bachelor Degree, Artificial Intelligence Engineering Degree.
The Department of Information Technology Engineering offers a master degree, the Master of IT Engineering (MITE), which upgrades skills and knowledge of students in information and telecommunication technologies to a specialist level for a variety of ICT careers ranging from programmers/software developers, IT consultants, project managers in IT, network specialists to wireless and mobile engineers.
Graduates of the Department of Information Technology Engineering can work as: Agile Software Developer, Agile Web Developer, Agile Mobile Developer, Fullstack Developer, Network & Security Engineer, Data Engineer.

## ITE bachelor degree overview

The Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE) trains students in software, computer organization and mathematics, with elective courses such as Artificial Intelligence, Computer Graphics, Game Development, Data Mining, and Security.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), students are required to do practicum from year 2 to year 4 with real world problems.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), students must go for an internship in private or public organizations from the end of the third year.

## ITE grading, GPA and graduation

Assessment of study will be performed at the end of each semester. Evaluation will be shown as the Grade Point Average (GPA) (4.00 scale) and the Cumulative Grade Point Average (4.00 scale).
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 85–100 earns grade A (Excellent), worth 4.0 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 80–84 earns grade B+ (Very Good), worth 3.5 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 70–79 earns grade B (Good), worth 3.0 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 65–69 earns grade C+ (Fairly Good), worth 2.5 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 50–64 earns grade C (Fair), worth 2.0 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of 45–49 earns grade D (Poor), worth 1.5 grade points.
In the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), a score of <40 earns grade E (Fail), worth 0.0 grade points.

## ITE final semester options: internship, thesis or coursework

In the final semester (fourth year, Semester II) of the Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE), students choose between two options.
Option 1 of the ITE final semester is coursework plus a Project Report, worth 17 credits.
Option 2 of the ITE final semester is an Internship (Hard Skill) and Thesis, worth 14 credits, and it requires a GPA above 3.5.
To do the internship and thesis option in the ITE degree, a student needs a GPA above 3.5.
The Bachelor of Information Technology Engineering (ITE) totals 158 credits with Option 1 or 155 credits with Option 2 (computed from the curriculum).
The ITE degree requires 158 credits in total with Option 1, or 155 credits with Option 2.
ITE Option 1 is worth 17 credits and ITE Option 2 is worth 14 credits.
The ITE internship and thesis (Option 2) is worth 14 credits.
The ITE Option 1 courses are Fin Tech (3 credits); Artificial Intelligence (3 credits); Introduction to Cryptography (3 credits); Professionalism Computing & Ethics (3 credits); Project Report (5 credits).
