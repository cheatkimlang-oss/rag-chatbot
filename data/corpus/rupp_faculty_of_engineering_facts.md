## Faculty of Engineering programs

The bachelor programs of the Faculty of Engineering are Mechanical Engineering, Business & Supply Chain Analytics, Bioengineering, Food Technology Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence Engineering.
The Faculty of Engineering web page lists no master programs; RUPP's master and PhD programs are listed on the university's Graduate Programs pages.
The departments of the Faculty of Engineering are Information Technology Engineering, Telecommunication and Electronic Engineering, Bio-Engineering, Automation & Supply Chain Systems Engineering, and Environmental Engineering.
The Faculty of Engineering web page lists five departments, while the Dean's message says the faculty has four departments and seven degree programs.

## Faculty of Engineering teaching framework

The teaching framework the Faculty of Engineering uses is CDIO (Conceive, Design, Implement, Operate).

## Faculty of Engineering skills and English

The soft skills the Faculty of Engineering teaches are communication, team work, problem solving, and innovation and creativity.
Engineering students study English for 4 hours per week for 3 years and receive a certificate of completion.
The hard skills the Faculty of Engineering offers are Telecommunication & Electronic Engineering, Information Technology Engineering, and Bio-Engineering.
