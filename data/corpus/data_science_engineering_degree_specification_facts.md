# Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE): facts

Generated from the Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE) specification. DSE stands for Data Science and Engineering.

## DSE Program Overview & Introduction

DSE core objectives: Deliver rigorous training in programming, statistical analysis, scalable cloud infrastructure, and intelligent systems design.
DSE hands-on practicum: Students complete multi-semester Project Practicum sessions starting from Year 2 through Year 4, developing working prototypes, data pipelines, and intelligent software to solve industry challenges.
DSE institutional support: Curriculum frameworks and advanced research collaborations are supported via international partnerships including KOICA and GIST (Republic of Korea).

## DSE research domains

The research domains of the Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE) are: Web and Cloud Technology, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Big Data, Business Intelligence.
In DSE, the research domain Web and Cloud Technology covers Scalable cloud-native architectures and distributed systems.
In DSE, the research domain Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning covers Applied ML algorithms, predictive models, and intelligent agents.
In DSE, the research domain Big Data covers Distributed computing frameworks, ETL pipelines, and web mining.
In DSE, the research domain Business Intelligence covers Process optimization, marketing metrics, and data-informed decision automation.

## DSE careers and jobs

DSE graduates can get jobs as: Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst (BI Analyst), Associate Data Engineer, Data Engineer, AI / Machine Learning Engineer (AI/ML Engineer).
Graduates of the Bachelor of Data Science and Engineering (DSE) are qualified for roles including Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst (BI Analyst), Associate Data Engineer, Data Engineer, AI / Machine Learning Engineer (AI/ML Engineer).

## DSE final semester tracks

In the final semester (fourth year, Semester II) of the DSE program, students choose one of 3 tracks: Course Work Track, Research Thesis Track, Extended Training Track.
The DSE final semester tracks are Course Work Track, Research Thesis Track, Extended Training Track.
The DSE Course Work Track is designed for students completing structured coursework alongside their final capstone project.
The DSE Research Thesis Track is tailored for high-achieving students conducting an academic research project under direct faculty supervision.
The DSE Extended Training Track is designed for students participating in a full-semester cooperative training program embedded within external companies or industry partner organizations.
The DSE specification does not state a GPA requirement for any of the final semester tracks.

## DSE Foundation Year courses
